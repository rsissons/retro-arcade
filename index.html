RETRO ARCADE

Fourteen 80s classics. No quarters required.

INSERT COIN
Keyboard: arrow keys to move, Space to fire, P to pause, M for sound, Enter to start.
iPad and phones: on-screen buttons show up automatically. City Shield is tap to fire. The two racers also have tilt steering (tap TILT at the top).
Save to home screen: in Safari tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
High scores are kept on each device.
Every game except Zork is an original rebuild, made as a tribute to the arcade classic it's named after, and uses none of the original code or artwork.
Zork I is the real 1980 Infocom game, released under the MIT license by Microsoft in 2025 (source).
Music and sound effects are public domain (CC0) from OpenGameArt, by Juhani Junkala, Alex McCulloch and Spring Spring.
